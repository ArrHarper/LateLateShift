John · Pick #5

B+ Roster Grade

The most balanced roster in the league, but no strong positional dominance.

Bucky Irving for an 8th and Hockenson for a 16th is a +146 keeper haul — third-best in the league and the quiet foundation of everything here. The draft on top of it was streaky: four real steals in the first nine rounds built a starting lineup that projects fourth-best in the league, and then the double-digit rounds gave a chunk of it back with two of the night's biggest reaches.

The keepers did the heavy lifting — +146 of equity for the price of an 8th and a 16th — and the early draft stacked real value on top. The result is the fourth-best starting lineup in the league with genuine depth at every position. What keeps it out of the top tier isn't a weakness, it's the absence of a monster: Derrick Henry leads this roster, and five managers have someone better. Balanced enough to beat anybody, short of the single week-breaking player that separates the top three. A cage well-built; mind the door.
