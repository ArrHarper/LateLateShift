Eat the W

Colin · Pick #1

A Roster Grade

The league's best WR room, undercut by an RB group ranked 8th and roughly half the league average.

The top of this draft is genuinely strong — Ja'Marr Chase at the top, two kept starters at real discounts, and the second-best starting lineup in the league. Then the back half happened: deep sleepers taken four rounds before anyone else was thinking about them. It looks reckless on the board and costs almost nothing in practice — every one of those overpays was for a bench slot, while the picks that filled the lineup came in at +54, the best in the league.

The second-best starting lineup in the league, built on Chase — the single most valuable starter anyone here will put on the field — a discounted Kittle, and two of the best quarterback values of the night. There's also a bench assembled like a lottery ticket collection, bought at scratch-off prices rounds before anyone would have competed for them. The raw board score says this was the worst draft in the league; the starting lineup says it was one of the best. The difference is that the overpays all landed on players who won't play. That's a survivable mistake until it isn't — this depth chart can't absorb a single injury, and two bye weeks were scheduled disasters the moment the draft ended.

Keepers: The Quiet Compounders

A-

Keeper · Round 8

George Kittle TE · SF +45 picks of value

Year two of this keep, and the discount has only grown: a top-40 player for an 8th-round cost. Elite tight ends age slowly, and the market keeps forgetting it — Kittle is the fourth-best keeper value in the league this season.

Keeper · Round 5

Tee Higgins WR · CIN +8 picks of value

A fair-price keep rather than a bargain — Higgins costs a 5th and would go about a round earlier in an open draft. The real payoff isn't the discount, it's the stack: he and Chase now share a locker room and a lineup.

Draft Picks: Boom, Bust, Repeat

A-

✓ Best Pick

Baker Mayfield QB · Round 10

Fell 42 picks past his league price — a top-8 fantasy quarterback last season, acquired in Round 10 while the room chased upside elsewhere. The best quarterback value in the draft, and it wasn't close.

✗ Worst Pick

Cedric Tillman WR · Round 14

Taken 69 picks before his league price — the single biggest reach in the entire LLS draft. A Round 14 flier on a player the market had outside its top 200. When the discount rack is this deep, paying retail for Cleveland's WR2 is a choice.
