Wraith's Voidwalkers

Uzoma · Pick #10

B Roster Grade

Top-three at both QB and WR, offset by below-average RB and TE rooms.

Jayden Daniels for a 13th-round pick is a +99 discount — the single biggest keeper value in the league — and Brian Thomas Jr. for a 10th added +75 more. That's +174 of equity before draft night started, best in the league, and the draft protected it: fair prices for eleven straight rounds, then late steals at quarterback when nobody was watching. First in keepers, third in draft value, not a single wasted pick. What it adds up to is nine solid starters and no one to build a week around — the void walked quietly, and quiet is exactly the risk.

This is what a complete offseason looks like: the league's best keeper haul, a draft disciplined enough to protect it, and three startable quarterbacks in reserve. The grade lands where it does for one reason — every starter here is good and none of them is a problem for anyone else. Eight managers walk into September with a better top-of-roster player. Balance wins the weeks where everyone plays fine; December is decided by the guy who drops 35 on his own, and this roster is trusting Nico Collins to become him. The Voidwalkers never made a mistake. The open question is whether never making a mistake is the same thing as winning.

The best keeper value in the league, full stop: a top-3 fantasy quarterback kept for a 13th-round cost. Everyone else paid real capital at the position or streamed it; this roster got elite QB production for a pick most teams spend on a kicker.
