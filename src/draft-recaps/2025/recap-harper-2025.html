Live By McCaff Die By Muh Calf

Harper · Pick #4

C+ Roster Grade

A top-four RB room and nothing else above league average, including the worst TE room.

Two premium receivers were locked in at a 5th and a 9th before the night started, so this draft had one job: build cheaply around a finished core. Instead it paid early at every turn — five picks went past the preference window — and the only bargain of the night was a backup running back in Round 13. The keepers still grade an A. The lineup they were supposed to anchor finishes last in the league.

The keepers did their job: two stud receivers for a 5th and a 9th is an A haul in any season. The draft never converted that head start into anything — five reaches, one steal, and a starting lineup that projects last in the league. An A in July and a C+ in September is the whole story: this grade is the roster, and the roster is McCaffrey plus hope. Everything rides on six players staying healthy, and the most important one has his medical history in the team name. If the calf holds, none of it matters.
