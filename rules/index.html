Defensive Scoring

We use modified defensive scoring to better represent the in-game impact of a defense. The main goal is to reduce the chance that a defense can score big in fantasy while getting destroyed in real life. The first few scores against a defense have a smaller penalty, later scores have a greater penalty, and the overall max penalty for points allowed is increased. There is also an added penalty for 400 and 500 yards allowed.

ⓘ The max penalty for a DST is −7 pts (35 pts and 500+ yards allowed).

Keeper Rules

  • Up to 2 keepers per team — keeping players is optional.
  • Players drafted in Round 1 cannot be kept. This keeps a healthy pool of elite players in the draft mix each year.
  • The same player can only be kept for two consecutive seasons, at which point they return to the draft pool.

Keeper Costs

The cost to keep a player depends on if and where they were last drafted.

  • Keeper cost equals the player's most recent draft position — a player selected in round 4 in 2021 costs a 4th-round pick to keep in 2022.
  • Keeping a player a second consecutive season costs one round more — that 4th-round selection now costs a 3rd-round pick.
  • Any player not in the most recent draft is a UDFA: an 8th-round pick the first year, a 7th-round pick the second.
Example · drafted player
  1. 2020 — Mark Andrews drafted in round 6
  2. 2021 — Andrews costs a 6th-round pick to keep
  3. 2022 — Andrews costs a 5th-round pick to keep again
  4. 2023 — Andrews returns to the draft pool
Example · UDFA
  1. 2020 — Justin Jefferson acquired as a UDFA
  2. 2021 — Jefferson costs an 8th-round pick to keep
  3. 2022 — Jefferson costs a 7th-round pick to keep again
  4. 2023 — Jefferson returns to the draft pool

Keeper FAQ

What if I want to keep two UDFAs, or two players drafted in the same round?

The cost for the second keeper increases by one round. Keeping two UDFAs costs an 8th and a 7th; keeping two players drafted in the 10th round costs a 10th and a 9th.

If I only keep a player for one season, can I pick him again in the draft?

Yes — once a player returns to the draft pool, drafting him back onto your roster resets his keeper costs.
