Nacua Matata

Price · Pick #2

A- Roster Grade

Best QB room and second-best TE room, built on the second-worst RB group in the league.

Bijan at two and Josh Allen at nineteen is as clean an opening as a draft can have, and the keeper pair — Nacua and McBride — arrived at real discounts. From there the philosophy became clear: take your guy, whenever, at whatever the sticker says the price isn't. Six picks went well past the preference window, more than any roster this side of the league's true reachers. The board hated most of draft night. The lineup that came out of it is the third-best in the league.

Nearly every pick after Round 4 ignored the board's prices — six reaches against two steals — and unlike most overpaying in this league, these were not bench darts: the picks that filled this starting lineup came in at −51, the worst mark in the league. That's what the draft grade is measuring. What saves the roster is the top of it — Bijan, Allen, Nacua and McBride is a core four that would headline anyone's team, and it carries a lineup that projects third-best. Paying over the odds for the right players is a different sin than paying over the odds for the wrong ones. This draft committed the forgivable version. No worries is a great motto until Week 8, when four starters go on bye at once.

Draft Picks: Vibes Over Value

C-

✓ Biggest Value

Josh Allen QB · Round 2

Through ten rounds this draft never found a real bargain — Allen, three picks past his league price, is the closest it came. That is not larceny, it is paying about retail for the quarterback attached to the highest implied scoring offense in football. Every genuine discount on this roster arrived after round ten, which is a different kind of draft.

✗ Biggest Reach

Dylan Sampson RB · Round 10

Taken 50 picks before his league price — a rookie committee back grabbed in Round 10 while proven players sat on the board. The steepest of this draft's six reaches, and the clearest case of drafting the ranking you hope for instead of the one that exists.
