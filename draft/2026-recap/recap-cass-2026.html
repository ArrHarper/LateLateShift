Rice rice baby

Cass · Pick #8

B- Roster Grade

Third-best RB room and a solid WR trio undercut by the lowest-rated QB room in the league.

Cass drafted good players and paid about the right price for most of them — the projection grades this draft B+ on the quality of the names, the market grades it C+ on the prices. The running back room is the 3rd-best in the league behind Achane, Etienne and a David Montgomery steal, and the receiver trio is solid. What sinks the grade to a B- is the quarterback slot: Trevor Lawrence was kept at an 8th-round cost above his market price, and the projection has him barely above a quarterback you could grab off waivers. It's the lowest-rated quarterback room in the league.

The good half of this roster is genuinely good: Achane, Etienne and Montgomery make the 3rd-best running back room in the league, and the receiver trio of London, Rice and McConkey is a fair starting group. The bad half is one slot, but it's the one that scores the most: Lawrence at an 8th-round cost is the league's only keeper overpay and anchors its lowest-rated quarterback room. The draft fixed nothing there — Baker Mayfield 17 picks past his price in round 16 is the backup, and the projection isn't kinder to him. Week 8 benches Montgomery, Kittle and Etienne at once. Fix the quarterback and this lineup jumps; leave it and the backs are carrying dead weight every week.

Keepers: One Discount, One Overpay

B

Keeper · Round 8

Trevor Lawrence QB · JAC ≈ 1.5 rounds overpaid

An 8th-round cost on a quarterback the market takes around pick 88 — 15 picks above his price. The only keeper in the league that cost more than the player's market value. The projection is harsher still: barely above waiver level in a 15th-ranked Jaguars offense. This slot could have been a discount on someone else; instead it's the reason the keeper haul ranks 9th.

Keeper · Round 9

Travis Etienne Jr. RB · NO ≈ 5 rounds of value

A 9th-round cost on a back the market takes in round 4 — 51 picks of discount. He projects as a top-17 back in the Saints offense and starts as the second running back here. This is the keep that works: the running back room ranks 3rd in the league, and Etienne at a bench price is a big part of why.

Draft Picks: Right Players, Mostly Right Prices

B-

✓ Biggest Value

David Montgomery RB · Round 5

Fell eight picks past his league price in round 5 and starts as the third back. A goal-line back in a 20th-ranked Texans offense isn't glamorous, but he projects as a top-21 option, and getting him a round late — with Jadarian Price falling seven picks a round later — is how this roster ended up three deep at the position.

✗ Biggest Reach

Tank Bigsby RB · Round 12

Taken 42 picks before his league price in round 12, a bench back the projection doesn't rate. Tyjae Spears 24 early in round 14 and Kayshon Boutte 31 early with the last pick were the same kind of overpay. None of them cost a starter. George Kittle 11 picks early in round 7 is the reach that matters more — it bought the starting tight end, and the market had him two rounds later.
