Best roster
Price
Ranked 1st in points by position at every positional category other than RB and K, his 2 keepers were WR1 and TE1 respectively, and he got more waiver value than any other team in the league. Goes to show that in fantasy you can do everything right and still not make the 'bowl.

Worst injury luck
Ben
Two potential top-5 players lost to season-enders; Malik Nabers in wk 4, Tucker Kraft (on TE2 pace) in wk 9. Add in a gimpy CeeDee Lamb and 4-week IR stint for Pearsall. Just imagine having Nabers, Lamb, A.J. Brown, and Pearsall, and somehow ending up with the 4th-worst WR room in the league. Pain. Pure pain.
The Storage Unit
Worst keepers
Cass
Spending a 2nd rounder to keep Breece Hall, who was expected to go in the 3rd or 4th, was the injury. Spending a 5th to keep Lamar Jackson, projected as the QB1 but who ended the year as the QB20, that was the insult. The one-two punch of overspending and underperforming cements this as the year's worst pair of keepers.
The Toyota Camry
Aggressively adequate
John
The ultimate "B-" team. Fewest roster moves (7), minimal injuries, zero sauce. Highest league rank at any position group was 3rd, and drove an 8-6 season to the playoffs for a first-round exit. Paid the bills, showed up to work, made the playoffs. One of the teams of all time.
